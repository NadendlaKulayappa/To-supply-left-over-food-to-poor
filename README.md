Food Distribution Management System

A Salesforce-powered food redistribution platform designed to connect food donors, NGOs, and volunteers to reduce food waste and support underprivileged communities. The system streamlines food donation, request management, and delivery tracking through workflow automation and centralized data management.

📌 Project Overview

The Food Distribution Management System helps reduce food wastage by connecting food donors with NGOs and volunteers through a centralized Salesforce platform. The system manages donation requests, food collection, distribution tracking, and automated workflows to improve operational efficiency and ensure timely food delivery to needy communities.

📂 Project Workflow

1.	Donor submits food donation details
   
3.	NGO receives and verifies donation request
   
5.	Volunteers are assigned for pickup and delivery
   
7.	Food is distributed to needy communities
   
9.	Admin monitors activities through dashboards and reports
